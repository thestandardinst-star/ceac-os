# Go-Live Organisation Mapping

This file contains classification rules only. Do not commit private employee data.

- Staff Development: department/unit for staff training/development.
- Staff Chaplaincy: department/unit of the Staff Chaplain.
- Compliance: department/unit.
- Leadership Academy: programme, not department.
- Healing Streams: periodic campaign/programme, not department.
- Model Church: branch-church responsibility/scope, not office department.
- Wonder Church: branch-church responsibility/scope, not office department.
- OFGP Action Manager: staff/manager role collecting data from churches under the group; do not create an OFGP Action Manager department. Place responsibility under appropriate Group Pastor/secretariat context.

Authority rule: job title does not equal system authority. Head/Supervisor/Manager wording must not grant capabilities automatically. Use existing capabilities/memberships. Multiple department relationships use unit memberships instead of compound departments.

Existing valid accounts must be reconciled, not duplicated. For employees without email: do not fabricate email or auth users; retain roster mapping until Admin supplies an email and activates/onboards.

Production staff reconciliation must begin as DRY RUN showing identity matches, proposed new accounts, staff without email, unit memberships, job titles, authority changes and possible duplicates. Applying changes requires reviewed apply step.
